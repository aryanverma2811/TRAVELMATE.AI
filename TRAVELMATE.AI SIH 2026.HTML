About TravelMate.ai

Our Vision

TravelMate.ai is designed as a smart travel companion that brings trip planning, budget estimation, destination discovery, route optimization, weather awareness, safety support, transport planning and voice assistance into one platform.


The current single-file version works as a browser-based prototype. Destination and hotel information is curated into the app, while weather and map features can use public online services when available.
